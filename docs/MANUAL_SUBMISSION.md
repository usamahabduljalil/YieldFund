## Project description to review

YieldFund helps communities fund shared work through eligible yield donations. Supporters allocate tokens to an isolated campaign vault; realized eligible surplus goes to a fixed Sputnik DAO treasury, where council members approve payments. NEAR contracts enforce custody, asynchronous settlement, loss-aware withdrawals and separate campaign accounts. A caller-funded factory creates campaigns, and a separate verification DAO approves expiring attestations tied to immutable campaign metadata. Our testnet demo uses actual test tokens and a funded simulator: 30 tUSD donated, 20 paid after two council approvals, and 200 withdrawn with 800 remaining units backed by 800 tokens. Mainnet deployment, real Rhea yield and Trezu lifecycle verification are deferred. Unknown payment callbacks and partial creation remain fail-closed limitations; the hosted app is read-only.
